Overview

ShooterX is an indie action shooting game concept focused on fast-paced combat, smooth controls, and competitive arena gameplay mechanics. Designed for players who rely on quick reflexes, strategic positioning, and accuracy, it features real-time combat against AI and players.

The game includes diverse modes such as Deathmatch, Survival, Team Battle, and Capture the Objective, managed through optimized asset systems and physics-based projectile mechanics.

Key Features

  • Fast-paced physics-based shooting mechanics
  • Real-time competitive combat with diverse weapons
  • Dynamic maps with tactical cover and obstacles
  • Deathmatch, Survival, and Team Battle multiplayer modes
  • Responsive control schemes and leaderboard systems
